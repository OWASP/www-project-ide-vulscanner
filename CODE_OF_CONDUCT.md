# Code of Conduct

As an OWASP project, we are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for all contributors, users, and community members.

## 📌 Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and safe community, we pledge to:

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Use inclusive and respectful language
- Show empathy toward others

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Personal attacks or insults
- Disruptive behavior or trolling
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances

## 🙋 Enforcement

Violations of this code may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the project maintainers privately. All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly.

## 🤝 Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
